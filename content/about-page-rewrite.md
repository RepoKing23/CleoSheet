## It didn't begin in a spa. It began in nursing.

In 2013, Cleo started her career as a Registered Nurse, dedicating herself to patient care and developing a deep appreciation for the complexity of the human body.

Over time, that clinical foundation grew into a broader fascination with how health, confidence and appearance intersect. She saw how much a small, well-planned change could mean to someone, and how much it mattered that the person holding the needle understood far more than technique.

In 2022, she became a Nurse Practitioner, an advanced foundation from which to approach aesthetic medicine. Medical knowledge, aesthetic artistry, thoughtful assessment and human connection now shape every treatment she provides.

## A nurse practitioner first, a cosmetic injector second

Cleo's advanced clinical background influences every part of her approach. Her role is to assess, educate, guide, treat appropriately and care for you throughout the experience. Before any injections, she looks at:

- Medical history
- Facial anatomy
- Muscle movement
- Skin health
- Individual goals
- Treatment suitability

That order matters. Assessment comes first, and treatment is recommended only when it suits you. Sometimes the right advice is a smaller amount, a different treatment, or waiting.

## What makes an NP-led clinic different

As a nurse practitioner, Cleo works at an advanced scope of practice in Ontario. ## Your complimentary consultation at our Oakville clinic

Every client starts with a complimentary consultation. It is a relaxed conversation about what you would like to improve, your health history and what is realistic. There is no pressure to book a treatment on the day.

### What happens at your consultation

Cleo assesses your features, skin and muscle movement, explains the options that suit you, including when to do less, and gives you a clear plan with expected results, downtime and aftercare. You leave with the information to decide in your own time.

## Questions people ask before booking

### Is a nurse practitioner qualified to do cosmetic injections?

Yes. Nurse practitioners in Ontario have advanced training and an expanded scope of practice. At the clinic, every injection is assessed for and performed by Cleo, a nurse practitioner.

### What is the difference between a nurse injector and a cosmetic injector?

Both describe someone who performs cosmetic injections such as Botox and fillers. What matters is their training and scope. Cleo is a nurse practitioner, which means medical assessment is part of every treatment.

### Do I need to know which treatment I want before I book?

No. Most clients come in with a concern, not a treatment name. The consultation is where you work that out together.
